# Candidate personas and section order

Choose the primary persona from supplied experience and the target role. These are editorial starting points, not labels to print on a CV. Combine them where useful, such as an experienced manager changing industries.

| Candidate | Suggested section order | Evidence to emphasize | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Junior or recent graduate | Brief profile if useful; relevant skills; selected projects or internships; education; other experience | What was personally built, tested, deployed, or learned; project links when supplied; relevant paid or volunteer work | Presenting a course or personal project as professional employment, production scale, or a client engagement |
| Career switcher | Role-focused summary; relevant projects or retraining; transferable experience in reverse chronology; skills; education | Direct evidence in the new field, plus separately named transferable domain, customer, analytical, or delivery experience | Treating years in the previous profession as years in the target profession; hiding actual titles or dates |
| Experienced individual contributor | Professional summary; experience; relevant skills; education; selected projects if they add evidence | Depth, complexity, architecture, ownership, collaboration, reliability, and supported outcomes | Equating influence or mentoring with direct people management; exhaustive tool inventories |
| Manager or executive | Professional summary; leadership experience; relevant technical or operational capabilities; education; optional public work | Organizational scope, people leadership, delivery, product/business partnership, and technical judgment | A skills-first page that buries leadership; invented budgets or company-wide impact; aspirational titles as employment titles |
| International applicant | Use the relevant experience-based order above | Target location, languages, relocation preference, and eligibility only when explicitly supplied and relevant | Inferring nationality, visa status, language proficiency, or eligibility from a name, employer, school, or location |

Within Experience, retain reverse chronology. Use a short Selected Projects section to surface relevant work rather than reordering employers to imply a different career history. Remove empty sections. Use one page when the evidence fits comfortably; use a second page when relevant experience justifies it. Do not pad a junior CV or compress an executive CV into unreadable type.

## Head of Engineering and senior leadership review

Assess these dimensions against the actual posting when supplied. Without a posting, label this a provisional rubric; a company name or broad title does not establish its current hiring requirements.

| Dimension | Useful evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People and organization | Direct versus indirect reports, managers managed, hiring, feedback, performance, organizational changes | Technical leadership alone does not establish line-management or management-of-managers experience. |
| Delivery and reliability | Scope of delivery responsibility, operating practices, incidents, quality improvements, before/after measurements | On-call participation does not establish ownership of the entire reliability program. |
| Technical judgment | Architecture decisions, tradeoffs, modernization, security or operational decisions personally owned | Do not claim hands-on depth across every technology used by the company. |
| Product and business partnership | Decisions with product, commercial or customer stakeholders; impact tied to the candidate's work | Company ARR, funding, growth, or an acquisition is context unless the candidate's contribution is supported. |
| Operating responsibility | Confirmed budget, vendors, planning, compliance, hiring capacity, or cross-team coordination | Do not infer budget ownership from seniority or responsibility for a team. |
| AI and domain relevance | Specific AI-product, ML-platform, AI-adoption, or domain work and the candidate's role | Employment at an AI company alone does not establish model development or ML expertise. |

Prioritize the two or three dimensions most material to the role. Surface missing scope as a question, not an automatic rejection. Include concrete technical evidence in an executive CV when it supports judgment or credibility; avoid either a technology dump or an unsupported strategy-only narrative.
