# LinkedIn optimization

Work from the profile text or the sections actually accessible through the requested browser task. Keep the CV and profile consistent while allowing LinkedIn a more conversational voice. A missing or inaccessible section is unknown, not a flaw in the profile. Do not claim to have reviewed the whole profile from a headline, search snippet, or partial screenshot.

## Review in this order

1. **Target and evidence:** reuse the target role and confirmed experience already provided. Separate the user's own claims, verified facts, and missing confirmation. A target job's requirements do not become candidate skills.
2. **Positioning:** identify whether the headline and opening About lines make the candidate's role, relevant domain, and strongest supported contribution clear. Avoid generic superlatives and keyword lists that hide the actual story.
3. **Consistency:** compare titles, employers, dates, scope, and metrics with the supplied CV. Surface material discrepancies for confirmation rather than silently choosing the more impressive version. Aspirational titles must not appear as past employment titles.
4. **Experience:** prioritize evidence-backed ownership and outcomes. Use natural job-related terms where the candidate has the experience. Keep technologies and keywords truthful; do not promise search ranking or recruiter reach.

## Useful output

Lead with up to three prioritized improvements and one sentence explaining each. Then provide the requested replacement text:

- **Headline:** offer two or three distinct options built from a supported role, relevant domain, and credible focus. Avoid unsupported claims such as "thought leader" or "expert."
- **About:** open with the candidate's work and value, support it with one or two concrete examples, and close with their stated direction. Preserve their voice and requested language. Do not invent personal motivations or imply they are job-seeking if they have not said so.
- **Experience:** rewrite the most relevant supplied bullets, with a short explanation of what changed. Keep the candidate's contribution distinct from the team's result.

For a request about one section, keep the answer to that section. Put `Requires confirmation` items outside paste-ready text. When facts are missing, use a truthful lower-specificity version now and ask at most one or two focused questions for the next pass. Do not fill a draft with unconfirmed metrics or sample facts.

## Browser and saving

Follow [browser research](browser-research.md) when access is needed. Pasted text is a complete starting point and does not require connecting a browser. A review produces suggested text. Editing the live profile, sending messages, or changing account settings requires the user's specific request and the host's applicable controls.
