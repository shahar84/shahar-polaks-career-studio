# Privacy guidance

When requesting career material, briefly invite removal of unnecessary sensitive details if no reminder has already been given. Continue useful work on material already provided. Do not ask for identity documents, passwords, banking information, medical records, or unrelated sensitive data. Do not make unverified claims about platform retention, deletion, encryption, or confidentiality.

Use fictional fixtures for demonstrations and tests. Never save real candidate material into the plugin's examples, templates, repository, or regression fixtures. Keep links, contact details, and career facts out of public plugin documentation unless they belong to an explicitly authorized public example.
