# PDF design guidelines

The Classic, Crimson, and Warm PDFs in `assets/cv-design-examples/` are visual references only. Learn only single-column flow, date gutter, hierarchy, spacing, restrained sections, bullets, footer treatment, two-page distribution, and color restraint. Never reuse their factual content.

Classic is the default. Crimson and Warm are optional late-stage themes. Check page breaks, orphan headings, clipping, indentation, links, font size, blank pages, duplicates, and text-extraction order.
