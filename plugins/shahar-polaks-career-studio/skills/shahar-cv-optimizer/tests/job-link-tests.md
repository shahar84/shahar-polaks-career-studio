# Job and fit-review tests

- Accessible job URL: extract explicit requirements and preferences, note source/date, and classify material requirements against candidate evidence. Use only warranted categories, not a forced row for every label.
- Expired, inaccessible, or authenticated URL: disclose the limitation, request pasted text when needed, and still offer useful CV review from supplied material.
- Israeli role: use direct, evidence-based language without unsupported outcomes or regional personality stereotypes.
- Ambiguous market: continue useful fit analysis and neutral wording. Do not infer jurisdiction from salary currency or working hours. Ask about country only if it materially changes the recommendation.
- CV plus job description with a short review request: provide a qualitative verdict, decisive evidence, gaps, and next action without an unsolicited full CV, LinkedIn rewrite, or strategy package.
- Broad Head of Engineering request without an exact posting: clearly label the leadership rubric as provisional; do not invent the employer's requirements.
- Request a CV tailored to a specific vacancy without supplying the vacancy: ask for the posting, pasted description, or its requirements. Reuse the existing CV. Do not claim a generic draft is already tailored to an unknown job.
- Supply a posting and a thin CV: choose one or two questions from the highest-impact unanswered requirements, such as personal ownership of a production service and hands-on queue work. Ask before presenting a supposedly final targeted CV. Do not assume a technology, result, or leadership scope to fill a gap.
- Supply rich evidence in Hebrew for a specific vacancy: produce the final CV in English, emphasizing supported evidence relevant to that vacancy. Do not repeat intake, ask for an already supplied target, or ask about language when no override was requested.
- Answer earlier discovery questions: update the evidence inventory and use the answers in the CV. Stop asking once enough relevant evidence exists; do not restart discovery or insist on metrics that were explicitly unavailable.
- Decline further questions and request a draft: deliver the strongest supported draft with genuine gaps or tailoring limits described briefly outside candidate copy. Never invent missing facts, add placeholders, or require a discovery interview against the user's request.
