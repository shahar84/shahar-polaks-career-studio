# Six-phase methodology

Use these phases internally and proportionally to the request. They are not six required report sections.

1. **Target job:** read the vacancy and candidate material. Ask for the specific posting when missing; with no vacancy available, establish a target role and label the eventual CV as a role-focused baseline outside the artifact.
2. **Requirement and evidence mapping:** identify the few decisive requirements, distinguish required from preferred, select the relevant persona internally, and link each requirement to supported evidence or a material unanswered question.
3. **Focused discovery:** ask one or two job-specific questions per round, reuse answers, and uncover personal ownership, relevant examples, depth, scope, and outcomes. Distinguish missing proof from explicitly absent experience. Stop when enough evidence exists; skip redundant intake when the supplied material is already sufficient.
4. **Tailored English content:** read the selected content and layout templates, then choose the summary, section emphasis, and concise bullets for this job using supported actions, scope, and outcomes. Default to English regardless of conversation language; honor explicit output-language requests. Preserve real titles and chronology.
5. **Recruiter readability:** place the most relevant evidence early and verify logical reading order without exaggeration or keyword stuffing.
6. **Quality and delivery:** check evidence, consistency, extraction and rendering when files are created. Deliver the requested scope and keep unresolved questions outside candidate copy.
