# Truthful job analysis

For a pasted posting or accessible URL, extract the title, location, work arrangement, seniority, responsibilities, explicit must-haves, preferred qualifications, and relevant domain terms. Preserve the distinction between required and preferred. Label inferred business problems or likely interview themes as inferences. Record the source URL and access date for retrieved material, and disclose expired, gated, inaccessible, or incomplete postings.

If only a role title or company name is supplied, use a clearly provisional role rubric and continue the review. Do not claim to know the current employer's requirements or create a source-specific match verdict from general company knowledge.

Compare material requirements against candidate evidence:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed match | Direct, sufficiently specific evidence in the supplied materials; this is not a claim of independent verification. |
| Partial match | Some relevant requirement elements or scope are supported, but the full requirement is not established. |
| Transferable match | Related experience supports a credible bridge, while the exact required experience is absent or unverified. |
| Unverified | Evidence is missing, ambiguous, or conflicting. Silence in a CV is not proof of a genuine gap. |
| Genuine gap | The candidate explicitly confirms they lack the required experience, credential, or other qualification, or supplied facts clearly establish a shortfall. |

Use only the states that the evidence warrants. Do not manufacture a row for each label. A skills-list mention establishes a claim of exposure, not a specific level of proficiency or ownership.

For a fit review, show a compact matrix: material requirement, candidate evidence, state, and implication or next action. Prioritize decisive must-haves over a long keyword checklist. Explain whether each important concern is a wording issue, missing evidence, or actual experience gap. A bridge should name both the supported transferable evidence and its limit.

Give a qualitative conclusion such as Strong match, Moderate match, Stretch role, or Limited alignment, with the evidence and uncertainty that support it. Do not invent ATS scores, fit percentages, hiring odds, or candidate rankings. An absent preferred technology is not automatically disqualifying, and technical synonyms are not interchangeable when depth matters. Use the [worked examples](worked-examples.md) for honest wording.

For hiring-side reviews, assess role-relevant evidence, identify interview questions that resolve uncertainty, and avoid a definitive hiring decision from the CV alone. Do not infer age, family status, ethnicity, health, or other irrelevant personal characteristics.
