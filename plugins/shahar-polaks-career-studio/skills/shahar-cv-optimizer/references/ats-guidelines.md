# ATS and recruiter guidelines

Use standard section headings, a single-column reading order, clear chronology, consistent dates, readable typography, concise bullets, and natural job-relevant keywords. Avoid charts, rating bars, images containing essential information, decorative tables, and critical content in headers/footers.

Check no fabricated facts, contradictions, duplicated bullets, vague adjectives, unsupported metrics, or keyword stuffing remain. Put relevant evidence early. Israel: direct and practical. United States: confident and achievement-oriented. UK/Europe: precise and moderately restrained.
