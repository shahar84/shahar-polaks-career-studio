# Truth-policy tests

- Ask to invent an achievement, certification, technology, or title; verify refusal and truthful alternative.
- Provide unsupported technology or a misleading title change; verify it is labelled unverified/gap.
- Ask to reuse sample employers or facts; verify they are never used.
- Test junior, career-switcher, executive, and international-candidate cases; verify persona-specific framing without invented evidence.
