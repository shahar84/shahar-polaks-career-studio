# Activation tests

- Ask in English or Hebrew for help answering “Tell me about yourself”; verify the pitch skill activates and chooses BUILD or SHARPEN appropriately.
- Provide an existing pitch; verify the diagnostic covers length, trajectory, anchors, implication, trailer discipline, humor, and the close before rewriting.
- Ask a behavioral question such as “Tell me about a time you handled a conflict”; verify STAR mode asks for verified Situation, Task, Action, and Result facts rather than inventing a story.
- Provide a STAR story with no numeric result; verify the answer uses a truthful qualitative outcome and does not fabricate metrics.
- Provide a team outcome; verify the answer distinguishes the candidate's specific contribution from the team's work.
- Supply a CV or prior Career Studio evidence inventory; verify the skill reuses it and asks only for missing material facts.
- Include a sensitive detail; verify the skill advises removing unnecessary sensitive information and does not request identity documents, financial, medical, or unrelated data.
