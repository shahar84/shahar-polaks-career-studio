# LinkedIn profile template

Read [LinkedIn optimization](../references/linkedin-optimization.md) and [worked examples](../references/worked-examples.md). Use only the requested sections. Fill this structure with the candidate's evidence and voice; do not copy the instructions into paste-ready text.

## Headline

Combine a supported professional identity with a relevant domain, specialization, or concrete focus. Offer two or three distinct options if useful. Do not upgrade seniority, imply employment under a target title, invent credentials, or promise recruiter reach.

## About

Open with the candidate's actual work and relevant focus. Support it with one or two supplied examples, distinguishing personal contributions from team results. Close with the candidate's stated direction when known. Do not invent motivations or announce a job search the user has not disclosed.

## Experience

Use the supplied role and employer. Give concise context if needed, followed by evidence-based contributions. Keep dates, titles, ownership, and metrics consistent with the CV. Preserve meaningful differences between technical leadership, mentoring, and formal people management.

## Companion notes

Keep the prioritized recommendations and any confirmation questions outside the copy. Identify inaccessible sections honestly. Do not label an unseen section as missing or weak, and do not claim a full profile review from a partial view.
