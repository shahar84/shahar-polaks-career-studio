# Privacy information

Career Studio helps with CVs, LinkedIn profiles, job matching, and interview preparation. These tasks can involve personal career information, including employment history, education, achievements, contact details, and application plans.

## What the package contains

This package contains instructions, reference material, templates, design examples, and a Browser app dependency. It does not configure an author-operated server, a custom analytics service, or a database for collecting candidate materials.

That describes this package, not the data practices of ChatGPT, Codex, Claude Code, Browser, a connected service, or a website you visit. Messages, uploads, generated files, and connected-tool activity are handled by the host and relevant services under their own settings and policies. This document makes no claim about their retention, deletion, training use, confidentiality, or encryption.

## Share only what the task needs

You can work from pasted text, selected CV sections, or a full document. Remove unnecessary contact details and other sensitive information before sharing. The workflow does not require identity documents, passwords, banking information, medical records, or unrelated sensitive data.

Sample CVs and demonstration stories are examples, not evidence about you. Confirm any inferred or uncertain facts before using them in a professional document.

## Browser-assisted tasks

When requested and supported by the host, Career Studio can read relevant profile, job, and company pages through available research or browser tools. Keep the request scoped to the pages needed for your task. Complete any necessary sign-in through the host's supported browser flow, not by placing credentials in chat.

A review produces recommendations. Sending a message, saving a profile change, uploading a CV to a website, or submitting an application requires a specific user instruction and the host's applicable approvals. Information sent to another site is subject to that site's practices.

## Support and feedback

GitHub issues in this public repository can be read by other people. Share a redacted example or fictional reproduction, not a CV, private profile text, application record, or credential. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for the details that help diagnose an issue without sharing candidate information.
