- Hebrew: ask how to begin or give a vague request for career help; verify the exact Hebrew onboarding, four starting paths, privacy reminder, and invitation to reply with 1–4.
- Provide a CV, job description, job URL, LinkedIn profile, or a specific task; verify the full onboarding message is skipped and the relevant workflow begins directly.
- Upload/paste a CV, job description, job URL, or ask for ATS/LinkedIn/interview help; verify activation.
- Supply partial information; verify a useful draft plus focused confirmation questions.
- Use a scanned PDF or photo; verify the model attempts to read it visually rather than refusing outright, never claims guaranteed-accurate extraction or "OCR," flags transcribed names/dates/numbers as needing confirmation, and asks for pasted text if reading it reliably isn't possible.
