# Geographic market guidance

Use the supplied employer instructions, job location, target country, application format, and user preferences before applying general conventions. Use Israel as a working default when no market signal exists, without assuming the candidate lives there or has eligibility there.

Keep the baseline portable: direct wording, supported outcomes, standard headings, readable chronology, and essential professional contact information. Avoid personal details that do not help the application. Do not infer nationality, work authorization, relocation willingness, or language proficiency.

Ask about country only when it materially changes a recommendation, such as a supplied application requirement, qualification naming, document size, or eligibility wording. If country is unclear, continue the evidence review and use neutral English; salary currency and time-zone overlap alone do not establish a jurisdiction. Do not block useful work while waiting for a market answer.

For English documents, use consistent spelling and date formats suited to the known audience. Use A4 for the bundled visual references; adapt to Letter when the user or destination requires it. Check current official requirements if the task involves a specific legal or eligibility rule. Do not present broad regional personality stereotypes as recruiting facts.
